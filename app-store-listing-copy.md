Ready to paste into Google Play Console and Apple App Store Connect once your developer accounts are approved.

## Google Play Console

**App name** (30 char max)
Pocket Finance

**Short description** (80 char max, shown under the name in search/browse)
Swipe through breaking market news. No prices, no trades — just the story.

**Full description** (4000 char max)

Pocket Finance turns market news into a fast, swipeable feed — built for how you actually scroll.

WHAT YOU GET
• A vertical, swipeable news feed pulled from real financial publishers, refreshed continuously
• Swipe right on any story to see the company behind it — plain-language business info, recent headlines, and related companies
• AI-written "Pocket Briefing" summaries that boil down what happened, why it matters, and the takeaway — for readers who want the gist fast
• Sentiment tags so you can see the tone of coverage at a glance
• Follow companies you care about to boost them in your feed
• Save and like articles to build your own reading library
• Explore trending tickers, industries, and regions
• Comment and react on stories (optional account required)

WHAT THIS APP IS NOT
Pocket Finance is a news app. It does not show live stock prices, execute trades, or manage your money. Nothing in the app is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice — sentiment tags reflect the tone of news coverage, not a prediction. Always do your own research.

PRIVACY
We collect only what's needed to run your account — no ad tracking, no data sales, no advertising in the app at all. Full details at pocketfinance.app/privacy.

Read Pocket Finance in guest mode with no account, or sign up to save, like, comment, and follow companies.

**Category:** Finance
*(News & Magazines is a reasonable alternate if Finance underperforms in testing — worth an A/B via Play Console's store listing experiments once live.)*

**Tags/keywords to consider in metadata:** finance news, stock news, market news, swipe news, business news feed

**Privacy Policy URL:** https://www.pocketfinance.app/privacy

**Data safety section (Play Console form) — what to declare:**
- Collects: email address, user-generated content (comments/reactions), app activity (articles read, likes, saves, follows)
- Shared with third parties: No (Marketaux receives no personal data; Clarity analytics is anonymized, no email/password/content)
- Data encrypted in transit: Yes
- Users can request deletion: Yes — in-app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) and via email
- Ads: None

**Content rating questionnaire — heads up:**
The app has user-generated comments (with profanity/spam filtering + reporting). Play's IARC questionnaire will likely land this at "Everyone" or "Teen" depending on how you answer the UGC exposure questions — answer honestly; don't guess "Everyone" if unsure, since a mismatch risks a takedown later.

**Age rating questionnaire — heads up:**
Same consideration as Play Store: user-generated comments typically push Apple's age rating to 12+ (under "Infrequent/Mild" User-Generated Content) rather than 4+, even with moderation in place. Answer the questionnaire honestly rather than defaulting to the lowest rating.

**App Review notes (optional but recommended given this is a live-URL wrapper):**
Consider adding a note in the "App Review Information" field explaining the app loads pocketfinance.app inside a native shell with native splash/status-bar/icon — Apple's reviewers sometimes flag pure web wrappers under Guideline 4.2 (Minimum Functionality). Worth pointing out the native-feeling swipe gestures, offline splash/icon, and that this is functionally equivalent to how many major news apps (and Twitter/X, Reddit, etc.) ship their web experience natively.
