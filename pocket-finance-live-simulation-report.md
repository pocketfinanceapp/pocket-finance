# Pocket Finance — Live App Simulation Report

**Date:** July 21, 2026
**Environment:** Production (pocketfinance.app/home), tested via live browser automation
**Method:** Simulated vertical scroll (For You + Trending feeds) across roughly 85 feed cards in one continuous session, swipe-left (article panel), swipe-right (business info panel), console/network monitoring throughout, plus a direct check of the infinite-scroll endpoint.

## What's working

Vertical scrolling through the For You feed advanced cleanly through 85+ articles with no repeat of the earlier "reset to top on the third card" bug — that fix is holding. The Trending tab loads correctly and visibly tags its lead story with an orange "TRENDING" badge. Swipe-left opens the full article reader reliably: headline, byline, source, real hero photo, ticker chip, sentiment tag, and the "Pocket Briefing" AI summary all rendered cleanly on every article tested. Swipe-right opens the business info panel just as reliably — company logo, name, ticker, Follow button, description, Founded/HQ/Industry facts, a Wikipedia link, aggregate sentiment, and a recent-headlines list.

## Issues found

**Infinite scroll doesn't actually trigger in production.** This is the most important finding from the extended run. I scrolled well past the point where the "load more when 5 articles remain" logic should have fired — and it never did. Zero calls to `/api/news/more` were recorded (checked both the network log and the browser's own performance timeline) over 85+ cards of scrolling. I confirmed the backend endpoint itself works fine: calling `/api/news/more?page=2` directly returned 33 real articles. So the bug is specifically in the client-side trigger in `NewsFeed.tsx` — the effect that's supposed to watch `remaining <= 5` and fetch more either isn't running, isn't recomputing `remaining` correctly, or its guard conditions are blocking it even in the plain "For You" view. Practically, this means once a user finishes the initial ~60-article batch, the feed likely just stops rather than continuing to serve fresh articles — worth root-causing before this becomes a visible "feed ran out" complaint from real users.

**Recurring hydration error.** React error #418 (a client/server render mismatch) fired 9 separate times over the ~30-minute extended session — roughly once every 2 minutes, correlated with scrolling/card transitions. This was previously marked fixed (task #120), so it's either regressed or the original fix only handled one of several cases. It doesn't visibly break the UI, but it means something is rendering different content on the client than the server sent, which is worth tracking down properly rather than leaving as background noise.

**Duplicate/repeating articles.** "Kroger: The Business Is Improving, But The Earnings Outlook Is Still Murky (NYSE:KR)" appeared twice in the For You feed early in the session, and "AIHS and CECC Form Joint Venture..." also resurfaced later after already having been seen. Both repeats happened well within the initial ~60-article batch, so this isn't a pagination duplicate — it points to the personalization/ranking logic occasionally re-surfacing articles the user has already scrolled past. (This finding is now somewhat entangled with the infinite-scroll bug above: if the feed truly isn't extending past ~60 articles, repeats could also simply mean the same finite pool is being re-ranked and re-shown rather than the app running out of fresh content to display — worth checking both angles together.)

**Mismatched images and source attribution on wire/PR articles.** At least five press-release-style articles (OKYO Pharma, an AIHS/CECC joint venture notice, a Defense Metals conference notice, an Iridex product announcement, a Seer proxy-fight notice) all rendered the same unrelated photo sourced from `manilatimes.net`, a Philippine news outlet. On closer look this time, the byline itself also read "Manilatimes" for the AIHS/CECC story — so it's not just a stray image, the source/publisher field is being attributed to whichever outlet happened to republish the wire release, with a generic thumbnail that outlet uses for syndicated content. That's a plausible explanation for why the same photo appears across unrelated headlines, but it still means readers see a misleading byline and a generic stock photo instead of the original wire source. Worth deciding whether to filter these out, prefer the original wire source over the republishing outlet, or accept it as a known Marketaux data quirk.

**A dead hero-image link.** One article ("Banque Saudi Fransi," source Mubasher) pointed to an image URL that returned a 503 twice in a row (including on the app's own automatic retry). The card presumably fell back to placeholder art correctly, but it's a sign that some fraction of "usable" image URLs from Marketaux are actually dead links server-side, not just low-quality ones — a case the existing image filter doesn't currently catch since it checks the URL shape, not whether the asset actually loads.

**Minor: a truncated description with no ellipsis.** One card ("Buying Eversource Energy For Returns And Dividends") showed a description that just stopped mid-sentence — "Eversource Energy stock remains a" — with no trailing "…". Cosmetic, but looks like a bug rather than intentionally short copy.

**One gap in the transcript-placeholder fix.** The recent fix that swaps in fallback art for "earnings/conference call transcript" headlines has a hole: "HEXPOL AB (publ) 2026 Q2," a dry filing headline that doesn't match that exact phrase, still showed Seeking Alpha's generic sitewide placeholder image. The regex needs to be broadened to catch report/filing headlines generally, not just the transcript wording.

**A lot of very dry wire content.** SEC Form 8.3 filings, proxy statements, investor-conference RSVPs, and "save the date" earnings-call announcements clustered in consecutive feed positions during testing. Individually these are legitimate content, but stacked together they make the feed feel thin on real stories — worth a look at whether these should be deprioritized in ranking or filtered out more aggressively.

**Heavier-than-expected background API usage.** Every article the feed renders — whether or not the user opens its business panel — triggers a burst of calls: `/api/company-info`, `/api/marketaux/entity-news`, `/api/marketaux/entity-stats`, `/api/marketaux/similar`, and `/api/article-summary`, plus two Supabase `user_stats` round trips (a read then a write) per card. Over ~30 cards that's several hundred network requests, most for panels the user never opened. This is presumably intentional prefetching for a snappy swipe experience, but it's worth knowing this is the cost profile per active user — it scales linearly with scroll depth regardless of engagement, which matters for your Marketaux/Supabase usage tier as you get real traffic.

## Bottom line

The core interactions — scroll, swipe-left, swipe-right, Trending — all work correctly and feel solid across an extended 85+ card session. Nothing found here is launch-blocking today, but I'd prioritize three things before a wider marketing push: the infinite-scroll trigger not firing (this caps how much of your content real users can actually reach), the recurring hydration error (already "fixed" once and clearly isn't), and the manilatimes.net-style image/byline mismatch (the kind of thing a user notices and screenshots). The duplicate-article, dead-image-link, and dry-content-clustering issues are quality polish rather than urgent bugs, and the API volume point is just something to watch as usage grows.
